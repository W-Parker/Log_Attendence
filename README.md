# RollShift

The staff app for duty rosters and attendance.

## [Download RollShift 1.3.0](https://github.com/W-Parker/Log_Attendence/raw/main/RollShift.apk)

67M · Android 7 or newer

1. Open the link above on the phone and download the file.
2. Open the downloaded file and tap **Install**. If the phone asks, allow
   installing apps from your browser.
3. Open RollShift and enter your organisation's server address on the sign-in screen.

Already installed? The app tells you when a new version is out and installs it
for you — just tap **Update now**.
